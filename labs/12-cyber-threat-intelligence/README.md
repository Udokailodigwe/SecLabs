# Lab 12 — Cyber Threat Intelligence

**Prepared by:** udodigwe  
**Classification:** Public-source analytical research  
**Framework:** MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise

This portfolio examines publicly documented cyberattack campaigns, translating threat intelligence into actionable SOC detection, investigation and response priorities. Findings distinguish reported activity from analytical inference and unresolved intelligence gaps.

| Case study | Incident period | Threat actor | Primary impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01 — MOVEit Transfer](reports/01-MOVEit-Cl0p/README.md) | May–June 2023 | Cl0p / TA505 | Data theft and extortion |
| [02 — SharePoint ToolShell](reports/02-SharePoint-ToolShell/README.md) | July 2025 | Storm-2603 | Warlock ransomware and enterprise compromise |

## Research competencies

- Open-source intelligence collection and source evaluation.
- Threat actor profiling, vulnerability analysis and attack-chain reconstruction.
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping with evidence-based confidence ratings.
- Indicator analysis, threat hunting and incident response prioritization.
- Intelligence gap identification and technical/executive reporting.

These are historical campaign assessments, not victim-specific forensic investigations. References are included in each report and should be revalidated before operational use.
