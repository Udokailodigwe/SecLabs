# Lab 15 — Digital forensics and Autopsy

**Objective:** Collect live forensic artifacts, acquire a Linux disk image and validate its integrity for offline investigation.

**Environment:** Hyper-V, Alpine Linux and Kali Linux.

**Tools:** Autopsy, The Sleuth Kit, dd, lsblk, sha256sum and netstat.

**Result:** Acquired a 10 GiB disk image, generated SHA-256 and MD5 fingerprints, identified three partitions and passed Autopsy MD5 integrity validation.
